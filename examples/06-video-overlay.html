How layering works

Each frame draws the scene first (regular canvas ops — gradients, particles, lines), then drawElementImage() stamps the caption and the player chrome on top. Each overlay element keeps its DOM box for clicks: tapping play toggles state, dragging the volume slider works, clicking the track seeks.
