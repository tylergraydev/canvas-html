How the form gets onto the ball

The orb body, the rotating wireframe (meridians + parallels projected from real 3D coordinates), and the glass highlight are all native canvas 2D. The form is laid out as a flat DOM subtree. To paint it onto the sphere we treat it as a texture: a patch of the sphere's surface is divided into columns, each column's corners are rotated and tilted with the globe and projected to the screen, and the matching slice of the form is drawn into that quad as two affine-mapped triangles via drawElementImage(). Columns facing away from the viewer are culled, so the form curves around the limb and disappears over the horizon exactly like printing on a real ball. A final transparent draw at the patch centre pins the live DOM hit-box so the fields stay typeable while the form faces front.
