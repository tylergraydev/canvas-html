How the 3D rolling works

Every frame, gl.texElementImage2D() uploads the textarea's rendered pixels into a GPU texture. A subdivided quad mesh wraps the texture around an invisible cylinder — the vertex shader takes each (u, v) on the paper and turns it into a position on the cylindrical surface. As lines accumulate, the mesh's wrap angle increases, so older lines visibly arc back over the platen.
