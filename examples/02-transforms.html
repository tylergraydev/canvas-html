What's happening

Inside onpaint we wrap drawElementImage() with ctx.translate → ctx.rotate → ctx.scale. The returned transform composes all of those operations, so feeding it back into card.style.transform keeps the DOM hit-box exactly under the painted pixels — try clicking a colour pill while the card is mid-spin.
