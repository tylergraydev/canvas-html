Why this works

The CSS perspective and rotation live entirely in the DOM subtree. The canvas just calls drawElementImage() on the wrapper — whatever the browser would paint for that subtree (including 3D transforms) ends up on the canvas. Hit-testing uses the wrapper's post-transform geometry, so the button stays clickable in 3D.
