How the mirror works

We call drawElementImage() twice each frame. The first draw is upright and its returned transform is fed back into panel.style.transform for hit-testing. The second draw flips the Y axis via ctx.scale(1, -1) and is masked with a top-to-bottom gradient using destination-in compositing.
