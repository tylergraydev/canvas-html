How the eyes find the caret

A hidden mirror <div> sits behind the textarea with identical font, padding, and width. On every input or selection change we rebuild its content as before + <span class="caret-marker"> + after. That marker's getBoundingClientRect() is the caret's screen position. From there it's a simple atan2() from each eye to the marker.
